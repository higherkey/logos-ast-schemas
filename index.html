Defensive Open-Core Infrastructure for Tabletop RPGs

Project Logos is a specialized Game Master Engine and administrative co-pilot designed to eliminate mechanical friction in tabletop RPG gameplay without encroaching on human creativity. Large Language Models are restricted to probabilistic intent parsing, while an immutable Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) logic core executes mechanical rules with 100% mathematical precision — guaranteeing zero slop and zero hallucinations.

Core Mandates & Philosophy

Guiding architectural principles and ethical mandates behind Project Logos.

1. The "No Slop" Mandate

Human artistic authority remains primary. Generative AI is strictly restricted to probabilistic intent parsing — mechanical rules adjudication is always 100% deterministic.

2. Bring Your Own Key / Model (BYOK / BYOM)

Individual users can connect local engine instances to their own LLM providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama). Zero platform tax — creators only pay direct compute costs to their chosen provider.

3. Proprietary Engine Platform & Open Rules Ecosystem

High-value engine orchestration code remains secure and proprietary, while all ruleset data schemas, hit point trackers, and hit threshold AST files remain completely open-source and vendor-lock free.

4. Physical-to-Digital Protocol

Rejects predatory double-purchase models by enabling physical book owners to unlock digital AST schemas at no extra cost.

Project Roadmap & Milestones

Strategic milestones for the evolution of the Project Logos ecosystem.

MILESTONE 1 • ACTIVE

Open Rulebook AST Catalog & 11-System Cohorts

Establishing community-driven, open-source AST schemas for major tabletop RPG playstyle cohorts (PbtA, Forged in the Dark, Pathfinder 2e SRD, d20 SRD) and validating sandboxed mathematical execution trees.

MILESTONE 2 • PLANNED

Automated Rulebook Ingestion & Player Studio

Ingesting physical rulebooks directly into playable AST schemas using Neuro-Symbolic (NeSy) visual document parsing models and connecting them to first-party player character tools with Git-backed campaign timelines.

MILESTONE 3 • FUTURE

Publisher Ecosystem & Multi-System Federation

Partnering with commercial publishers for official storefront distribution channels, self-refining telemetry nodes, and expanding multi-system cross-play capabilities.
